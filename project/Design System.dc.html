02 — Status language

Incident states

Every incident lives in exactly one of three states. Color is consistent everywhere it appears — badges, table rows, filters, charts.

Open

Awaiting review. Neutral but attention-drawing — these are the administrator's queue.

Confirmed

Verified as a genuine violation. Positive resolution — eligible for evidence export.

Discarded

Reviewed and dismissed as a false positive. Muted and de-emphasized, but retained.
